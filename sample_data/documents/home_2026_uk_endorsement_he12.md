This endorsement applies to Jigsaw Home Insurance policies issued under policy wording version 2026.1 (UK) that start or renew on or after 1 July 2026, where endorsement HE12 is shown on the schedule. All other terms, conditions and exclusions of the policy remain unchanged.

## 1. Escape of water excess

The compulsory excess for any claim for loss or damage caused by escape of water is changed from £350 to £450. This replaces the escape of water excess shown in the Excesses table of the policy wording. Any voluntary excess shown on your schedule still applies in addition.

## 2. Trace and access

The limit for trace and access to find the source of escape of water under Section 1 - Buildings is increased from £5,000 to £10,000 for any one claim.

## 3. Leak detection condition

If you notice signs of a possible leak, such as damp patches, a drop in boiler pressure or an unexplained increase in your water bill, you must arrange for the leak to be investigated within 14 days. If you do not, we will not pay for damage that could have been prevented had the leak been investigated within that time.

## 4. Winter precautions

The winter precautions in the Unoccupancy condition now apply if your home is unoccupied for more than 3 days in a row between 1 November and 31 March, instead of 7 days.
