# Purpose and authority

This guide sets out the acceptance criteria, referral points and rating loads for Jigsaw Home Insurance (wording 2026.1, UK). It is for use by Jigsaw underwriting staff only and must not be shared with customers or brokers. Where a risk falls outside this guide, refer it before quoting.

## Underwriting authority levels

| Role | Maximum buildings sum insured | Maximum contents sum insured | Can accept referred risks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Underwriter | £750,000 | £100,000 | No |
| Senior underwriter | £1,000,000 | £150,000 | Yes, except declines |
| Underwriting manager | £2,500,000 | £250,000 | Yes |

Any risk with a buildings sum insured above £2,500,000 must be referred to the Head of Personal Lines Underwriting.

# Acceptance criteria

## Construction

Standard construction is brick, stone or concrete walls with a roof of slate, tile, concrete or metal. Non-standard construction is treated as follows:

| Construction | Decision |
|---|---|
| Timber frame with brick outer skin | Accept at standard rates |
| Flat roof covering up to 25% of the total roof area | Accept at standard rates |
| Flat roof covering more than 25% of the total roof area | Refer to a senior underwriter |
| Thatched roof | Refer to a senior underwriter; apply a 35% load if accepted |
| Prefabricated or system-built concrete | Refer; require a structural survey |
| Wholly timber or mobile homes | Decline |

## Listed buildings

Grade II listed (or category B or C in Scotland) properties can be accepted with a 15% load on the buildings premium. Grade I and Grade II* listed properties (or category A in Scotland) must be referred to the underwriting manager.

## Subsidence history

If the property has suffered subsidence in the last 10 years, or is within 50 metres of a property that has, refer to a senior underwriter. We will only consider acceptance if a structural engineer's report confirms that the property has been stable for at least 3 years after repairs. Accepted risks carry a subsidence excess of £2,500.

## Flood risk

Properties in a high flood risk area, or that have flooded in the last 25 years, must be referred. Properties built after 1 January 2009 are not eligible for the Flood Re scheme and should be quoted at full flood rates or declined.

## Occupancy

| Occupancy | Decision |
|---|---|
| Owner occupied, main residence | Accept |
| Let to a single family on an assured shorthold tenancy | Decline - refer to Jigsaw Landlord product |
| Holiday home or second home | Refer; unoccupancy conditions apply |
| Unoccupied for more than 30 days at inception | Refer to a senior underwriter |
| Business use beyond clerical work, or visitors to the home for business | Refer |

# Rating loads and discounts

## Claims history (last 5 years)

| Number of claims | Action |
|---|---|
| None | 10% claims-free discount |
| 1 claim | No load |
| 2 claims | 25% load on the affected section |
| 3 or more claims | Refer to a senior underwriter |
| Any escape of water claim over £10,000 | Apply endorsement HE12 at renewal |

## Security

Valuables over £20,000 in total require an intruder alarm installed to a recognised standard. Valuables over £50,000 in total, or any single item over £15,000, must be referred and may require a safe.

## Convictions

Any unspent criminal conviction (other than a motoring offence) for anyone living at the home must be referred. Convictions for arson, burglary, theft or fraud in the last 5 years result in a decline.

# Referrals

Refer risks using the underwriting referral queue in the policy administration system. Senior underwriters aim to respond to referrals within 1 working day. Quotes for referred risks are valid for 30 days.
