# About your car insurance

This policy wording, your schedule, your certificate of motor insurance and any endorsements together form your contract with Jigsaw Insurance Limited. Your certificate shows who can drive your car and what it can be used for. Your schedule shows your level of cover, your excesses and any optional extras you have bought.

This version (2026.1) applies to policies that start or renew on or after 1 January 2026 for cars registered and kept in the United Kingdom.

# Definitions

**Car**: The vehicle shown on your schedule and certificate, including its standard accessories and spare parts while fitted to or kept with it.

**Market value**: The cost of replacing your car with one of the same make, model, age, mileage and condition at the time of the loss, based on recognised trade valuation guides.

**Approved repairer**: A repairer in our network that we have authorised to repair your car.

**Young driver**: A person under 25 years of age at the time of the incident.

**Inexperienced driver**: A person who has held a full UK driving licence for less than 2 years at the time of the incident.

# Section A - Liability to other people

We will cover you for your legal liability for:

- death of or injury to any person, without limit;
- damage to other people's property, up to £20,000,000 for any one claim, including legal costs and expenses up to £5,000,000.

This cover also applies to any person driving your car with your permission, if your certificate allows them to drive.

## Driving other cars

If your certificate shows that you can drive other cars, you are covered for third party liability only while driving a private car that you do not own and that is not hired to you, provided that the owner has given permission and the car is insured in its own right. This extension applies only to the policyholder, only if they are aged 25 or over, and it does not cover damage to the car being driven.

# Section B - Fire and theft

We will pay for loss of or damage to your car caused by fire, lightning, explosion, theft or attempted theft. We will pay up to the market value of your car at the time of the loss.

We will not pay for loss or damage:

- if the keys or keyless entry device were left in, on or near the car while it was unattended;
- if the car was left unlocked, or with a window or roof open, while unattended;
- caused by someone who obtained your car by deception, for example a person posing as a buyer.

# Section C - Accidental damage

We will pay for accidental damage to your car, including damage caused by malicious damage and vandalism. We may repair the car, replace it, or pay the cost of the loss, up to the market value.

## New car replacement

If your car is less than 12 months old, you are its first registered keeper, it has covered fewer than 12,000 miles, and the cost of repair is more than 50% of the manufacturer's list price, we will replace it with a new car of the same make and model, if one is available in the UK.

## Recovery and storage

If your car cannot be driven after an accident, we will pay the reasonable cost of moving it to the nearest approved repairer or safe place, and storage charges for up to 28 days.

# Section D - Windscreen and windows

We will pay for the repair or replacement of broken glass in your windscreen, windows and sunroof, and for any scratching to the bodywork caused by the broken glass. A claim under this section only will not affect your no claim discount.

| Windscreen and windows claim | Excess |
|---|---|
| Repair using our windscreen supplier | £25 |
| Replacement using our windscreen supplier | £115 |
| Replacement using any other supplier | £250, and we will pay no more than £500 |

# Section E - Personal accident

If you, or your partner, are injured in an accident involving your car, and within 12 months the injury is the sole cause of the following, we will pay:

| Injury | Benefit |
|---|---|
| Death | £5,000 |
| Loss of a limb | £5,000 |
| Permanent loss of sight in one eye | £5,000 |

We will not pay more than £10,000 for any one accident, and we will not pay this benefit to anyone aged over 75 at the time of the accident.

# Section F - Personal belongings

We will pay up to £300 for personal belongings in your car that are lost or damaged by accident, fire or theft. We will not pay for money, credit cards, mobile phones, tablets, laptops, goods carried for business purposes, or items stolen from a convertible car while the roof is down.

## Child car seats

If a child car seat is fitted in your car at the time of an accident, we will pay the cost of replacing it with a new seat of a similar standard, even if there is no apparent damage. This cover is in addition to the personal belongings limit.

# Section G - Courtesy car

While your car is being repaired by an approved repairer after a claim under Section C, we will provide a small courtesy car, subject to availability, for the duration of the repairs. A courtesy car is not provided if your car is stolen and not recovered, or is declared a total loss, or while repairs are carried out by a repairer who is not an approved repairer.

# Excesses

You must pay the following excesses towards any claim under Section B or Section C. Excesses are added together where more than one applies.

| Excess | Amount |
|---|---|
| Compulsory excess | £250 |
| Additional excess for a young driver | £350 |
| Additional excess for an inexperienced driver aged 25 or over | £200 |
| Additional excess for a provisional licence holder | £500 |
| Additional excess if a repairer who is not an approved repairer is used | £250 |

Any voluntary excess shown on your schedule is added to these amounts.

## Uninsured driver promise

If your car is damaged by an uninsured driver, you will not have to pay any excess and your no claim discount will not be affected, provided you give us the make, model and registration number of the other vehicle and, where possible, the name and address of the other driver, and the incident was not your fault.

# Using your car abroad

Your policy gives you the minimum cover needed to meet the compulsory motor insurance laws of any country in the European Union, and of Andorra, Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway, Serbia and Switzerland. If you have comprehensive cover, we will extend your full cover to these countries for up to 90 days in any one period of insurance, provided that your main permanent address remains in the United Kingdom. You do not need a green card to drive in these countries, but you must carry your certificate of motor insurance.

# No claim discount

If you do not make a claim during a period of insurance, your no claim discount will increase at your next renewal. If you make a claim, your no claim discount will reduce at your next renewal, unless:

- the claim is for windscreen and windows only;
- the claim is under the uninsured driver promise; or
- we have recovered all our costs from someone else.

## Protected no claim discount

If your schedule shows that your no claim discount is protected, you can make two claims in any period of three years before your no claim discount is reduced. Protecting your no claim discount does not protect the overall price of your insurance.

# General exceptions

This policy does not cover any accident, injury, loss, damage or liability:

- while your car is being driven by anyone not allowed to drive by your certificate;
- while your car is being used for a purpose not allowed by your certificate, including hire or reward, ride sharing for profit, or food and parcel delivery, unless your certificate shows business use for delivery;
- while your car is being driven by someone who is under the influence of alcohol or drugs above the legal limit, or who fails to provide a sample when required to do so;
- while your car is being used on a race track, at a track day, or on any circuit, including the Nürburgring, or in any race, rally or speed test;
- caused by wear and tear, mechanical or electrical breakdown, or tyre damage caused by braking, punctures or cuts;
- caused by putting the wrong fuel in the car;
- caused by war, terrorism (except to meet the requirements of the Road Traffic Act), or radioactive contamination.

# General conditions

## Keeping your car in a roadworthy condition

You must keep your car in a roadworthy condition and have a valid MOT certificate where one is required by law.

## Telling us about changes

You must tell us immediately if there is any change to the information on your schedule or certificate, including a change of address, a change of car, a change of main driver, any modification that affects the car's performance or appearance, or any motoring conviction, fixed penalty or driving licence endorsement.

## Cancelling your policy

You can cancel your policy within 14 days of receiving your documents and we will refund the premium for the days you were not covered, provided no claim has been made. After the cooling-off period, you can cancel at any time and we will refund the premium for the remaining period, less an administration fee of £45. No refund will be given if a claim has been made in the current period of insurance. You must destroy your certificate of motor insurance when the policy is cancelled.

# Making a claim

You must report any accident, loss or damage to the accident and claims line as soon as possible, and within 7 days of the incident. You must report theft or vandalism to the police immediately and give us the crime reference number. Do not admit liability or offer to make any payment without our written consent. You must send us any claim form, summons or other legal document you receive, without replying to it.

If your car is a total loss, we will pay the market value of your car less the excess, and once we have paid the claim the car becomes our property. If your car is subject to a finance or hire purchase agreement, we will pay the finance company first.
