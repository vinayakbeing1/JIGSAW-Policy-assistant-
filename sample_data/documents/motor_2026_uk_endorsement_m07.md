This endorsement applies when M07 is shown on your schedule. It forms part of your Jigsaw Car Insurance policy, wording version 2026.1. All other terms and conditions remain unchanged.

## 1. Telematics device

A telematics device must be fitted to your car within 14 days of the start of the policy and must remain fitted and working at all times. You must not move, disconnect or tamper with the device. If the device is not fitted within 14 days, we may cancel your policy by giving 7 days notice.

## 2. Night-time driving by young drivers

If an accident happens between 11pm and 5am while your car is being driven by a young driver, an additional excess of £1,000 applies to any claim under Section B or Section C. This is in addition to all other excesses.

## 3. Driving score

We will review your driving score every 3 months. If your score falls below 40 out of 100 for two reviews in a row, we may contact you to discuss your driving and may change your premium at renewal.

## 4. Device faults

If the device stops working, you must tell us within 7 days and allow our engineer to repair or replace it. You will not be charged for the first replacement device.
