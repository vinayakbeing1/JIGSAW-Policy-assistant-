# About your travel insurance

This policy wording and your certificate of insurance form your contract with Jigsaw Insurance Limited. Your certificate shows the type of policy you have bought (single trip or annual multi-trip), the people insured, the area of travel and any optional extras.

This version (2026.1) applies to policies bought on or after 1 February 2026 by residents of the United Kingdom.

## Who can be covered

To be covered, each insured person must live in the United Kingdom, be registered with a UK doctor, and have been in the UK for at least 6 of the last 12 months. Single trip policies are available to people aged up to 85. Annual multi-trip policies are available to people aged up to 79 at the start date of the policy.

## Trip limits

| Policy type | Maximum length of any one trip |
|---|---|
| Single trip | The trip dates shown on your certificate, up to 365 days |
| Annual multi-trip | 31 days, or 45 or 60 days if shown on your certificate |
| Annual multi-trip trips within the UK | Only covered if you have pre-booked at least 2 nights' accommodation away from home |

If a trip under an annual multi-trip policy is longer than the maximum trip length, there is no cover for any part of that trip.

## Emergency assistance

If you need medical help abroad, call Jigsaw Emergency Assistance on +44 20 7946 0300, 24 hours a day. You must contact us before you go into hospital as an in-patient, or as soon as possible afterwards.

# Section 1 - Cancellation and cutting short your trip

We will pay for travel and accommodation costs that you have paid or have agreed to pay and cannot get back, if you have to cancel your trip or cut it short because:

- you, a travelling companion or a close relative die, are seriously injured or become seriously ill;
- you are called for jury service or as a witness in a court of law;
- your home is seriously damaged by fire, flood or burglary within 7 days before you are due to travel;
- you are made redundant, provided you have worked continuously for the same employer for at least 2 years;
- you or a travelling companion are diagnosed with an infectious disease, including COVID-19, within 14 days before your departure date.

We will not pay if you cancel because you no longer want to travel, or because the Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office advises against travel to your destination after you booked, unless the advice is issued within 28 days before you are due to travel.

# Section 2 - Emergency medical and repatriation expenses

We will pay reasonable and necessary costs if you become ill or are injured during your trip, including hospital, medical and surgical treatment, emergency dental treatment to relieve pain (up to £350), and the cost of bringing you home if our medical advisers agree it is necessary.

## Pre-existing medical conditions

You must tell us about any medical condition that you have been treated for, or prescribed medication for, in the 2 years before buying this policy or before booking a trip. If you do not tell us and we have not agreed to cover the condition, we will not pay any claim arising from it. We may charge an additional premium to cover a declared condition.

## What is not covered

- Treatment that can reasonably wait until you return to the UK.
- Any trip you take against the advice of a medical practitioner, or to get medical treatment abroad.
- Claims arising from drinking alcohol to an extent that your judgement is seriously affected.
- Any costs in your home country, other than as part of repatriation.

# Section 4 - Personal baggage

We will pay for loss of, theft of, or damage to your baggage. We will pay the value of the item at the time of loss, after a deduction for wear and tear. The most we will pay for any one item, pair or set is £300, and the most we will pay for all valuables is £400.

We will not pay for:

- valuables left in checked-in luggage;
- baggage left unattended in a public place or in an unlocked vehicle;
- loss or theft not reported to the police within 24 hours, or to the airline before you leave the airport;
- mobile phones, unless the optional gadget cover is shown on your certificate.

# Section 8 - Travel delay

If your outbound or return international departure is delayed for more than 12 hours because of strike, industrial action, adverse weather or mechanical breakdown of your aircraft, sea vessel or train, we will pay £25 for each full 12 hours of delay, up to £250. If your outbound departure is delayed for more than 24 hours, you can choose to cancel your trip and claim under Section 1 instead.

# Sports and activities

Your policy automatically covers the activities listed below, as long as they are not your main reason for the trip, you follow the instructions of the organiser and you wear appropriate safety equipment.

- Hiking and trekking below 2,500 metres.
- Scuba diving to a depth of 18 metres if you hold a recognised qualification, or 9 metres if you are with a qualified instructor.
- Snorkelling, kayaking on grade 1 and 2 rivers, and surfing.
- Cycling, but not BMX or downhill mountain biking.

Winter sports, including skiing and snowboarding, are only covered if you have bought the winter sports option, which is shown on your certificate. Winter sports cover includes ski equipment up to £500, ski hire up to £200 and piste closure up to £300. Off-piste skiing is only covered when accompanied by a qualified guide.

# General exclusions

We will not pay any claim arising from:

- travel to a country or area where the Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office has advised against all travel or all but essential travel, at the time you started your trip;
- you taking part in manual work, unless it is voluntary work for a charity;
- riding a motorcycle or moped over 125cc, or riding without a helmet, or without the licence required in the UK to ride that vehicle;
- deliberately putting yourself at needless risk, except in an attempt to save human life;
- war, terrorism involving nuclear, chemical or biological weapons, or civil unrest you have chosen to travel into.

# Cancelling your policy

You may cancel your policy within 14 days of buying it or receiving your documents, whichever is later, and we will refund your premium in full, provided you have not travelled and have not made a claim. For annual multi-trip policies, there is no refund if you cancel after the cooling-off period.

# Making a claim

You must tell us about any claim within 31 days of returning home. Call the claims team on 0808 157 0310 or make a claim online. You must provide original receipts, a police report for any theft or loss, and a property irregularity report from the carrier for baggage lost or damaged in transit.
