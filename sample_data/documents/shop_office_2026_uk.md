# About this policy

Jigsaw Shop and Office Insurance is a package policy for shops, offices, salons and similar small businesses with up to 25 employees and an annual turnover of up to £3,000,000. This wording, your schedule and any endorsements form the contract between your business and Jigsaw Insurance Limited. Only the sections shown as operative on your schedule apply.

This version (2026.1) applies to policies that start or renew on or after 1 January 2026 for businesses in the United Kingdom.

# Definitions

**Business**: The business activities described in your schedule, carried out from the premises.

**Premises**: The buildings at the address shown in your schedule, and the land within their boundaries.

**Stock**: Goods held for sale, raw materials and work in progress, belonging to you or held in trust.

**Gross profit**: The amount by which turnover and closing stock exceed opening stock and the cost of purchases, as described in Section 3.

**Indemnity period**: The period starting from the date of the damage during which the results of the business are affected, up to a maximum of 12 months unless a longer period is shown in your schedule.

**Unoccupied**: Not used by you or your employees for normal business activities for more than 30 days in a row.

# Section 1 - Buildings

We will pay for loss of or damage to the buildings of the premises caused by fire, lightning, explosion, earthquake, storm, flood, escape of water, theft or attempted theft, riot, malicious damage, subsidence, and impact. The most we will pay is the buildings sum insured shown in your schedule. Shop fronts and fixed external signs are covered up to £10,000 under this section.

# Section 2 - Contents and stock

We will pay for loss of or damage to contents and stock at the premises caused by the same events as Section 1.

| Item | Limit |
|---|---|
| Contents and stock | The sums insured shown in your schedule |
| Stock seasonal increase in November and December | 25% of the stock sum insured |
| Tenant's improvements | £25,000 |
| Computer equipment | £15,000 |
| Theft of stock from the premises (forced entry only) | Stock sum insured |
| Tobacco, wines and spirits | £5,000 |
| Customers' goods held for repair or processing | £10,000 |

Theft is only covered where entry to or exit from the premises was gained by forcible and violent means.

# Section 3 - Business interruption

If your business is interrupted because of damage at the premises that is covered under Section 1 or Section 2, we will pay the loss of gross profit due to the reduction in turnover during the indemnity period, and the increased cost of working that you reasonably incur to keep the business running.

| Additional cover | Limit |
|---|---|
| Loss of gross profit | Sum insured shown in your schedule |
| Maximum indemnity period | 12 months, unless 24 months is shown on your schedule |
| Denial of access by the police or a public authority | £25,000 |
| Failure of public utility supply (more than 24 hours) | £25,000 |
| Loss of attraction from damage to neighbouring premises | £10,000 |
| Notifiable disease at the premises | £25,000, with an indemnity period of 3 months |

We will not pay for loss caused by a notifiable disease outside the premises, or by the closure of all businesses in an area by a government or public authority.

# Section 4 - Money and assault

| Money | Limit |
|---|---|
| Money in transit, or at the premises during business hours | £5,000 |
| Money in a locked safe at the premises outside business hours | £2,500 |
| Money at the premises outside business hours, not in a safe | £500 |
| Money in the home of a director or employee | £500 |

If you or an employee are injured during a robbery or attempted robbery while on business, we will pay £10,000 for death or permanent disablement, and £100 a week for up to 52 weeks for temporary total disablement.

Money must be banked at least once every 3 working days. We will not pay for shortages due to error or omission.

# Section 5 - Goods in transit

We will pay for loss of or damage to goods in transit in your own vehicles within the United Kingdom, up to £10,000 for any one vehicle. We will not pay for theft from an unattended vehicle unless all doors were locked, all windows closed, and any security device was set, or between 9pm and 6am unless the vehicle was in a locked building.

# Section 6 - Employers' liability

We will cover your legal liability for bodily injury to employees arising out of their employment in the business, up to £10,000,000 for any one claim including costs. Cover for claims arising from acts of terrorism is limited to £5,000,000. This section meets the requirements of the Employers' Liability (Compulsory Insurance) Act 1969, and you must display a copy of the certificate of employers' liability insurance where employees can read it.

# Section 7 - Public and products liability

We will cover your legal liability for accidental bodily injury to any person and accidental damage to property, arising in connection with the business.

| Cover | Limit of indemnity |
|---|---|
| Public liability | £2,000,000 for any one claim, or £5,000,000 if shown in your schedule |
| Products liability | £2,000,000 in total in any one period of insurance |
| Legal defence costs under health and safety legislation | £1,000,000 |

We will not pay for liability arising from the use of any motor vehicle, from work at a height of more than 10 metres, from the treatment of any person by a medical practitioner, from products exported to the United States of America or Canada, or from any work away from the premises involving the application of heat.

# Section 8 - Glass

We will pay for the cost of replacing broken fixed glass at the premises, including shop fronts, windows and glass doors, and the cost of temporary boarding up. The most we will pay for any one claim is £10,000, and £500 for lettering, alarm foil and signs on the glass.

# Excesses

| Type of claim | Excess |
|---|---|
| All property claims unless shown below | £250 |
| Escape of water | £500 |
| Subsidence, heave or landslip | £1,000 |
| Theft or attempted theft from premises without an approved alarm | £500 |
| Business interruption | Nil, other than the first 24 hours of interruption |
| Public and products liability (property damage only) | £250 |
| Employers' liability | Nil |

# Conditions

## Intruder alarm

If your schedule shows an intruder alarm condition, the alarm must be maintained under a maintenance contract with an approved installer, and set whenever the premises are closed for business. You must tell us within 24 hours if the alarm police response is withdrawn.

## Unoccupancy

You must tell us before the premises become unoccupied. Once the premises have been unoccupied for more than 30 days, we will not pay for loss or damage caused by escape of water, theft, attempted theft or malicious damage, and the excess for all other claims increases to £1,000.

## Waste and fire safety

Waste must be stored at least 5 metres from the buildings, in metal containers with lids or in a locked compound, and removed from the premises at least once a week. Portable fire extinguishers must be serviced every 12 months.

## Cancellation

You may cancel the policy within 14 days of receiving the documents and we will refund the premium in full, provided you have not made a claim. After that, we will refund the premium for the remaining period, less an administration fee of £50. We may cancel the policy by giving 14 days' notice in writing.

# Claims

You must tell us about any incident that may lead to a claim as soon as possible, and within 14 days for property claims. Report theft and malicious damage to the police immediately. For liability claims, you must send us any letter of claim or legal document unanswered within 7 days of receiving it, and you must not admit liability or negotiate a settlement without our written consent. Claims can be reported on 0808 157 0400.
