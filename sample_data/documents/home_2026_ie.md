# About your policy

Thank you for choosing Jigsaw Insurance. This policy wording, together with your schedule and any endorsements shown on it, forms the contract of insurance between you and Jigsaw Insurance Limited. Please read all three documents carefully and keep them in a safe place.

Your schedule shows which sections of cover you have chosen, the sums insured, your excesses and the period of insurance. Sections that are not shown on your schedule do not apply. Words shown in bold type in the original wording have the meaning given in the Definitions section.

This version of the wording (2026.1) applies to policies that start or renew on or after 1 April 2026 for homes in the Republic of Ireland. Jigsaw Insurance Limited is regulated by the Central Bank of Ireland for conduct of business rules.

# Definitions

**Buildings**: The structure of your home, including its fixtures and fittings, garages and outbuildings, swimming pools, tennis courts, drives, patios, terraces, walls, gates, fences and fixed fuel tanks, all within the boundaries of the land belonging to your home.

**Contents**: Household goods, valuables and personal possessions belonging to you or for which you are legally responsible, kept in your home. Contents does not include motor vehicles, caravans, boats, money, credit cards, or any part of the buildings.

**Escape of water**: Water escaping from a fixed water or heating installation, a washing machine, dishwasher, fridge freezer, water bed or fish tank.

**Excess**: The amount you must pay towards each claim. The compulsory excess and any voluntary excess are shown in your schedule and in the table of excesses in this wording.

**Home**: The private dwelling at the address shown in your schedule, used only for domestic purposes, other than a home office used for clerical work.

**Period of insurance**: The period shown in your schedule and any further period for which you renew the policy and pay the premium.

**Unoccupied**: Not lived in by you or a member of your family for more than 45 days in a row. To count as lived in, someone must sleep at the home on most nights.

**Valuables**: Jewellery, watches, items made of gold, silver or other precious metals, furs, pictures and works of art, and collections of stamps, coins or medals.

**We, us, our**: Jigsaw Insurance Limited.

**You, your**: The person or people named in the schedule as the policyholder, and members of their family who normally live with them.

# Section 1 - Buildings

## What is covered

We will pay for loss of or damage to your buildings caused by the following events during the period of insurance:

- fire, smoke, explosion, lightning or earthquake;
- storm or flood;
- escape of water or escape of oil from a fixed heating installation;
- theft or attempted theft;
- riot, civil commotion, strikes or malicious damage;
- subsidence, heave or landslip of the site on which your buildings stand;
- impact by aircraft, vehicles, animals or falling trees and branches;
- breakage or collapse of television aerials, satellite dishes and their fittings.

The most we will pay for any one claim under this section is the buildings sum insured shown in your schedule. The buildings sum insured cannot be more than €1,200,000 unless we have agreed a higher amount in writing.

## Additional buildings cover

These covers are included automatically and are in addition to the buildings sum insured:

| Additional cover | Limit for any one claim |
|---|---|
| Alternative accommodation if your home cannot be lived in | 20% of the buildings sum insured |
| Trace and access to find the source of escape of water | €6,000 |
| Accidental damage to underground services | €5,000 |
| Accidental breakage of fixed glass and sanitary ware | Buildings sum insured |
| Removal of debris and professional fees | Included within the buildings sum insured |
| Emergency access by the fire, police or ambulance service | €1,000 |
| Theft of keys and replacement of locks | €1,000 |

## What is not covered under Section 1

- Loss or damage caused by escape of water or theft while your home is unoccupied.
- Damage to the water or heating installation itself, other than the cost of trace and access shown above.
- Subsidence, heave or landslip damage to drives, patios, paths, walls, gates, fences, swimming pools or tennis courts, unless the main building is damaged at the same time by the same cause.
- Storm or flood damage to gates, hedges and fences.
- Damage caused by the settlement, shrinkage or expansion of new structures, or by coastal or river erosion.
- Loss or damage caused by wear and tear, gradual deterioration, rot, damp, condensation or a lack of maintenance.

# Section 2 - Contents

## What is covered

We will pay for loss of or damage to your contents while they are in your home caused by the same events listed under Section 1. The most we will pay for any one claim is the contents sum insured shown in your schedule. The standard contents sum insured is €80,000; you can choose a higher amount of up to €160,000.

## Limits that apply to contents

| Item | Limit |
|---|---|
| Any one valuable or personal possession (single article limit) | €2,500 |
| All valuables in total | One third of the contents sum insured |
| Money held for domestic purposes | €600 |
| Credit and debit card misuse | €1,000 |
| Contents in outbuildings and garages (theft only covered following forced entry) | €3,000 |
| Contents in the open within the boundaries of your home | €600 |
| Home office equipment used for clerical work | €5,000 |
| Garden plants, trees and shrubs | €1,000 |
| Food in a fridge or freezer spoiled by a change in temperature | €500 |
| Students' possessions while away at a university residence | €3,000 |

Items worth more than the single article limit must be listed separately on your schedule, otherwise the most we will pay for each item is €2,500.

## Seasonal increase

The contents sum insured is increased by 10% during December, and during the month before and the month after your wedding or civil partnership, to cover gifts.

## Alternative accommodation for contents

If your home cannot be lived in following an insured event, we will pay reasonable alternative accommodation costs for you and your domestic pets of up to €15,000 in total.

## What is not covered under Section 2

- Theft from an outbuilding or garage unless there was forced and violent entry.
- Loss or damage to contents while your home is unoccupied.
- Damage caused by domestic pets, including chewing, scratching, tearing or fouling.
- Loss of or damage to bicycles while left unlocked away from your home.
- Mechanical or electrical breakdown.

# Section 3 - Accidental damage (optional)

This section applies only if your schedule shows that accidental damage cover has been chosen.

We will pay for accidental damage to your buildings and contents. Accidental damage means sudden, unexpected and visible damage that has not been caused on purpose.

We will not pay for:

- damage caused by domestic pets;
- damage caused while your home is let or sublet;
- damage to clothing, contact lenses or mobile phones;
- damage caused by chipping, denting or scratching;
- the cost of repairing faulty workmanship or design.

# Section 4 - Personal possessions away from home (optional)

This section applies only if your schedule shows that personal possessions cover has been chosen. We will pay for loss of or damage to valuables and personal possessions that you carry or wear while away from your home anywhere in the world, for up to 60 days in any period of insurance.

| Item | Limit |
|---|---|
| Personal possessions sum insured | As shown in your schedule, up to €12,000 |
| Any one item (single article limit) | €2,000 |
| Money and travel tickets | €350 |
| Pedal cycles (unless listed separately) | €900 each |

We will not pay for theft from an unattended motor vehicle unless the item was in a locked boot or glovebox, out of view, and there are signs of forced entry.

# Section 5 - Liability

## Property owners' liability

As owner of your home, we will cover your legal liability to pay damages for accidental bodily injury to any person, or accidental damage to property, arising from your ownership of the buildings. The most we will pay for any one claim, including costs, is €2,600,000.

## Occupiers' and personal liability

As occupier of your home, and in your personal capacity anywhere in the world, we will cover your legal liability to pay damages for accidental bodily injury or accidental damage to property, up to €2,600,000 for any one claim including costs.

## Employers' liability for domestic staff

We will cover your legal liability for bodily injury to domestic staff employed by you up to €13,000,000 for any one claim.

## What is not covered under Section 5

- Liability arising from any business, trade or profession.
- Liability arising from owning or using motorised vehicles, aircraft, drones over 250 grams, or watercraft.
- Liability arising from any dog of a breed listed in the Control of Dogs Regulations 1998.
- Liability under an agreement or contract unless you would have been liable anyway.

# Section 6 - Home emergency (optional)

This section applies only if your schedule shows that home emergency cover has been chosen. Call the home emergency helpline before arranging any work.

We will pay up to €1,000 for any one emergency, including the call-out charge, labour, parts and materials, to resolve a sudden and unexpected event at your home that needs immediate action to:

- prevent damage or further damage to your home;
- make your home safe and secure; or
- restore the main heating, plumbing, drainage, electricity or gas supply.

There is no excess under this section. Boilers more than 15 years old are covered for the call-out and first hour of labour only.

# Excesses

You must pay the excess shown below towards each claim. If you have chosen a voluntary excess, it is added to the compulsory excess.

| Type of claim | Compulsory excess |
|---|---|
| All claims unless shown below | €300 |
| Escape of water | €400 |
| Subsidence, heave or landslip | €1,250 |
| Personal possessions away from home | €100 |
| Home emergency | Nil |
| Liability | Nil |

Voluntary excess options are €100, €250 or €500. Your schedule shows the voluntary excess you have chosen.

# General conditions

## Taking care of your home

You must keep your home in a good state of repair and take all reasonable steps to prevent loss or damage.

## Unoccupancy

If your home is going to be unoccupied for more than 45 days in a row, you must tell us before the 45 days have passed. We may then change the terms of your policy or ask you to take extra precautions. Between 1 November and 31 March, if your home is unoccupied for more than 7 days in a row, you must turn off the water supply at the mains and drain the water system, unless the central heating is set to keep the temperature at 10°C or higher.

## Security

If your schedule shows a security condition, all external doors must be secured with five-lever mortise deadlocks or multi-point locking systems, and all accessible ground floor windows must have key-operated locks. These must be in use whenever your home is left empty, and at night when everyone has gone to bed.

## Changes you must tell us about

You must tell us as soon as possible if:

- you change your address;
- you plan building work costing more than €60,000;
- the home will be used for business purposes other than clerical work;
- the home will be let or sublet; or
- anyone living at the home is convicted of, or charged with, any offence other than a motoring offence.

## Cancelling your policy

You may cancel this policy within 14 days of receiving your policy documents, or within 14 days of the start date, whichever is later (the cooling-off period). We will refund the premium in full provided you have not made a claim.

You may cancel at any time after the cooling-off period. We will refund the premium for the time left on your policy, less an administration fee of €35. We will not refund any premium if you have made a claim during the current period of insurance.

We may cancel this policy by giving you 14 days' notice in writing to your last known address, for example if you fail to pay your premium or provide information we have reasonably requested.

## Automatic renewal

We will write to you at least 21 days before your renewal date with the terms on which we are prepared to renew. If you pay by direct debit and you do not tell us otherwise, we will renew your policy automatically.

# Claims conditions

## How to make a claim

Please contact the claims helpline on +44 20 7946 0123 as soon as reasonably possible, and in any event within 30 days of discovering the loss or damage. You must:

- report any theft, attempted theft, malicious damage or loss of property to the police within 48 hours, and obtain a crime reference number;
- take reasonable steps to prevent further damage, for example by turning off the water supply;
- send us details of any claim made against you, and any letter or legal document, without replying to it;
- give us any information, receipts, valuations and proof of ownership we reasonably ask for.

## How we settle claims

We will decide whether to repair, rebuild, replace, or make a cash payment. For contents, we will replace items as new, except for clothing and household linen, where we will make a deduction for wear and tear. If we can replace an item through one of our suppliers but you choose a cash payment, we will pay no more than it would have cost us.

## Fraud

If you or anyone acting for you makes a fraudulent claim, we will not pay the claim, we may cancel the policy from the date of the fraudulent act and keep the premium, and we may recover any amounts already paid.
